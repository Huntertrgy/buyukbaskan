Being a chairman in Türkiye

From the amateur leagues to the Süper Lig, run your club from the chairman's desk.

Büyük Başkan puts you at the club's decision desk, not on the touchline. Found your club, answer to the members at the general assembly, and manage your ties with the terraces, the press, the city hall and the business world. No relationship ever buys a referee decision.

Answer to the members

Your annual report and budget go to the vote. Win approval and your mandate is renewed; lose it and your chair is at risk.

 Stakeholders

Your ties with the terraces, the press, the city hall, business and the federation open doors; no relationship ever buys a referee decision.

What does the player do?

The player acts as club chairman, managing targets, budget, staff, and sporting decisions. The game focuses on the long-term effect of decisions across a season.

Career saves, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on the device only; there is no Büyük Başkan account or automatic career-data upload to a developer server. The Android build may optionally use Google Play Billing and AdMob.

  • Squad and transfer decisions
  • Finance and sponsor tracking
  • Stadium and facility investment
  • News-driven decision outcomes
  • Match viewing and result review
