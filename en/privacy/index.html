Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current release build of Büyük Başkan handles data.

Short summary

Büyük Başkan does not create its own user account and does not send personal data to a developer server. Career saves and achievements stay on the device only. The Android build may optionally use Google Play Billing and AdMob.

Game saves, preferences, chairman profile choices, and achievement progress are stored in local device storage only.

  • No separate Büyük Başkan account system.
  • No automatic personal-data transfer to the developer.
  • Career saves and achievements are stored locally on the device only; there is no cloud save.
  • The Android build may optionally use Google Play Billing and AdMob.
  • The current Android manifest surface includes INTERNET and AD_ID permissions.

Data stored on the device

The app may store game progress, team preferences, audio and presentation settings, career saves, and similar game state in device-local storage such as IndexedDB and localStorage.

This data is used to run the game. The developer does not automatically receive it. All save and achievement data remains on the device.

  • Career progress and club state
  • Save slots
  • Language, audio, and display preferences
  • Chairman name, club name, stadium name, and in-game choices
  • Achievement progress (device-local only)

Ads and purchases

The Android build shows Google AdMob rewarded videos only, and only when you start one from Finance > Get Investment (up to 10 a season). There are no interstitial ads.

AdMob may process advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, and app interaction data on Google's side for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. The developer does not automatically transfer this ad data to its own servers.

In-app purchases (three investor cash packs and the Super Stadium) run through Google Play Billing once the products are active in Play Console.

Ad consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or another region where Google asks for consent, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) at start-up. There you choose whether ad data may be used for personalised ads. If you do not consent, rewarded videos may be served with limited, non-personalised ads. If no consent answer can be obtained (for example offline), no rewarded video opens.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. That entry appears only when Google requires the consent form; where it is not required, manage personalised ads in your device's Google ad settings.

  • Ad provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited.
  • Ad consent is collected with Google's consent form and changed in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Rewarded ad viewing is fully optional.
  • See Google AdMob privacy policy for ad data processing details.
  • When Billing is enabled, purchases run through Google Play.
  • In-game cash packs only affect the simulation balance.
  • There are no social follow or share rewards.

Crash reports (optional)

Settings > Send crash reports is off by default and appears only in builds with crash reporting configured. When you switch it on, a crash record and session counts are sent to Sentry over an encrypted connection when the app crashes.

A crash record holds the error name, the error message with numbers and quoted text removed, the stack trace, the app version and the screen path with ids removed. Technical device details such as the device model and OS version may be added. No personal data, no chairman, club or player names and no save data are sent.

  • Crash report provider: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.).
  • Switching it off stops sending at once.
  • Crash reports are not used for advertising or tracking.
