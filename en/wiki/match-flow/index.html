Match Day

The manager picks the team and the tactics; on match day you watch, and what you control is everything around the match.

Back to Wiki

Before kick-off

The match preview shows the two sides before the game. Use it to check the opponent and your own side, including unavailable or tired players. If the squad is short, empty places are filled from the youth squad before kick-off.

The live match

  • Kick Off to watch the match live, or skip straight to full time.
  • The score, clock and events play out on the Events tab; the Stats tab has the live stats and a momentum bar.
  • The screen shows the weather and the atmosphere in the stands.
  • You will see substitutions, VAR reviews, the referee's calls and the player of the match.
  • Set the match clock speed in Preferences (Fast, Normal, Slow or Very slow). It applies from the next match.
  • After the final whistle you can open all of the week's results and the league table, then continue to the news.
  • A red card brings a suspension; an appeal goes to the federation, which accepts or rejects it.

The crowd

How many people come to a home match depends on several things. The Fixtures/Results screen shows the crowd for every home match, and the Stadium screen shows your last home attendance against capacity.

Record crowds of 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000, 75,000 and 100,000 each unlock an achievement.

  • Capacity: the crowd can never be bigger than your ground, and a stand under construction is closed.
  • Ticket price: cheap tickets please the fans and fill the ground; dear ones strain both. You set the price through a news card each season.
  • The terraces: your relationship with the supporters' association moves home attendance by up to 3% either way.
  • Parking: open parking units raise matchday attendance.
  • Big stands: a finished stand over 9,000 seats lifts the base crowd by 3%, and over 15,000 by 6%.
  • Season tickets: season-ticket holders take part of the ground; the remaining seats stay on matchday sale.

Matchday income

Every home match books income on match day. Cup and European home ties earn gate money too, and away cup and European ties bring a share of the gate. See Finance for the full picture and Stadium for the outlets.

  • Ticket income from the gate. Every home match also pays 15% of the gate towards the stadium and running costs.
  • Food and drink and the club shop, if you have open units with enough capacity for the crowd.
  • Parking and the VIP lounge, earned per match.
  • Advertising boards, which bring board income per home match.
