Seasons, Promotion and Cups

Climb from the 1. Amatör to the Süper Lig, chase cups at home and in Europe, and build prestige season by season.

Back to Wiki

The league pyramid

The English game shows the league names in brackets. Your home city decides your group.

  • Süper Lig (Super League): 18 clubs. The top of the pyramid; the bottom 3 go down.
  • 1. Lig (First League): 20 clubs. The top 2 go up, plus a play-off for 3rd-6th; the bottom 4 go down.
  • 2. Lig (Second League): 2 groups of 18 (White, Red). Group winners go up, plus a play-off for 2nd-5th in each group; the bottom 3 per group go down.
  • 3. Lig (Third League): 3 groups of 18. Group winners go up, plus a play-off for 2nd-5th in each group; the bottom 2 per group go down.
  • Bölgesel Amatör (Regional Amateur): 2 groups of 18 (West, East). The top 2 go up, plus a play-off for 3rd-6th in each group; the bottom 3 per group go down.
  • Süper Amatör (Super Amateur): 2 groups of 18 (West, East). The top 2 go up, plus a play-off for 3rd-6th in each group; the bottom 3 per group go down.
  • 1. Amatör (First Amateur): 2 groups of 18 (West, East). The top 2 go up, plus a play-off for 3rd-6th in each group; nobody goes down.

After a promotion

The first season in a new league is a survival season: the league target is the last safe place, there is no fan target, a one-off promotion grant is paid (once per division), and staying up leaves fan approval at 50 or more. The bank also raises your overdraft limit.

Relegation keeps your prestige, but the relegation clause cuts first-team wages by 20%.

The cups

  • Turkish Cup: every club from the Süper Lig down to the Bölgesel Amatör enters, so a small club can draw the big ones. The two lowest amateur leagues play only the Amateur Cup.
  • League Cup, Anatolian Cup and Amateur Cup: further domestic cups, depending on your league.
  • Super Cup: the league champions against the Turkish Cup winners.

Europe

Who qualifies:

  • The top two of the Süper Lig play in the European Champions Cup.
  • The Turkish Cup winner plays in the European League, from any division. If the cup winner already finished in the top two, the European League place goes to 3rd.
  • Two more Süper Lig places go to the European Conference Cup.
  • In the league phase, 1st-8th go straight to the round of 16, 9th-24th play a knockout play-off and 25th-36th are out.
  • The Champions Cup and European League winners meet in the European Super Cup.
  • Reach the Champions Cup final, or win the Süper Lig in a season you play in the Champions Cup, to enter the World Club Summit (weeks 84-88).

The season report

The board sets four objectives each season: league finish, cup wins, club cash and fan approval. At the season close you get a grade. Meeting every target earns an A; an S needs more: finish 5 places above the fans' expectation, win the league or win a cup. The report also compares the season with the last one.

Prestige and titles

Every season earns 10 prestige plus your season score, with honour bonuses: 60 for a league title, 40 for promotion or a cup win, 20 for a play-off win, 15 for a cup final. First-season goals and the General Assembly add more.

Each title unlocks a new office ground, for looks only.

  • New Chairman: 0 prestige.
  • Local Chairman: 100.
  • Trusted Chairman: 250.
  • Builder Chairman: 450.
  • The City's Chairman: 700.
  • Renowned Chairman: 1,000.
  • National Figure: 1,400.
  • Club Legend: 1,900.
  • Football Giant: 2,500.
  • The Great Chairman: 3,200.
