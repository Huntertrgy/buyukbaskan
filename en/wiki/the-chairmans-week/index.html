The Chairman's Week

Büyük Başkan moves one week at a time: you decide through the news and move the week on from the home screen.

Back to Wiki

The home screen

  • Cash: the club's money. Spending comes out of it; match, sponsor and sales income goes into it.
  • Tiles: squad strength, the manager, the stadium and the season's profit or loss. Tap a tile to open it.
  • Season targets: what the board expects from you this season. Your end-of-season report is written against them.
  • First-season guide: in your first season, what to focus on next.
  • The main button: moves the week on. Matches are played and new posts arrive. Press it when you are ready.

The news

The news feed is the club's agenda. Anything that needs the chairman lands here as it happens. Some posts are just news; others ask for a decision, and those come to you one at a time. Every decision moves the club, so read the card before you choose. Typical decisions include:

  • Transfer targets, bids for your players and contract talks.
  • The ticket price, the season-ticket campaign, sponsor choices and the transfer budget.
  • The manager's requests: a team trip in a week without a game, a pay rise, advice to loan out, sell or release a player, or permission to talk to another club that wants him.
  • The players' bonus requests: a win bonus, a season target bonus or a cup bonus.
  • The fans asking you for an answer, and club incidents where you make the call.
  • Offers of help from city hall or the business circle, which come with a price (see Stakeholders).
  • The contract desk: each season it gathers expiring contracts in one card, with the manager's view next to each player. You can renew the manager's picks, decide one by one, or let them all go.

What changes from week to week

Your decisions and results move a set of indicators you can follow on the Chairman Power screen. Each indicator shows its last 5 weeks, and every move records its reason. The same screen holds your six stakeholder relationships.

  • Fan approval: how far the fans approve of you.
  • Trust in the manager: how much the fans trust the manager.
  • Manager happiness: how happy the manager is working with you.
  • Board trust: your average credit across the season objectives; partial progress counts.
  • Money also moves every week: income and expense rows are booked on match day and with the wage run, and a weekly finance report sums them up (see Finance).

Dates worth knowing

A few weeks of the calendar to remember:

  • The summer transfer window ends in week 10 and the January window in week 54 (deadline day is the last week).
  • Stand naming offers arrive in week 94.
  • The World Club Summit is played in weeks 84-88.
  • The youth intake joins in week 102.
  • The season then closes with your season report, and in assembly years the members' vote. See General Assembly and Seasons, Promotion and Cups.
