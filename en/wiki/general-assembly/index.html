General Assembly

Being chairman does not make the club yours alone: at the General Assembly the members vote on your annual report and your budget.

Back to Wiki

When it meets

  • The first ordinary assembly closes your second season. After that it meets at the close of every second season.
  • The members' dossier closes as the last two weeks of the season begin. From then on the vote uses the values on the forecast card, and nothing that changes afterwards moves the result.
  • The home screen and the season review show when the next assembly is due.

The vote forecast

Votes are not for sale: no option buys a vote, a match result or a referee decision. Only what you did counts.

Open the General Assembly screen (Vote, Parts and History tabs) to see your vote forecast with today's values. Board trust is read against your season objectives (league, cup, finances, fans); the objectives screen tells you what is needed. The vote is built from four parts:

  • Fan approval: 40%.
  • Board trust (the budget and annual report, from the season objectives): 25%.
  • Fans relationship: 15%.
  • City and business (the average of the council and business relationships): 20%.

The threshold and the outcomes

The threshold follows your board demands: 42 on Relaxed, 48 on Standard, 52 on Demanding.

  • Mandate renewed: the threshold plus 15 or more. The members back you by a wide margin. You gain 60 prestige, fan approval rises by 3 and every stakeholder relationship by 2.
  • Re-elected: at or above the threshold. You gain 30 prestige.
  • Survived by a whisker: up to 10 below the threshold. You stay, but the board is one step stricter next season, then returns to its old level.
  • You lost the vote: more than 10 below the threshold.

Losing the vote

A first loss is forgiven. You get two options:

  • Honourable resignation: the career ends here.
  • The board's offer, one more season: the new season has demanding targets, and the assembly meets again at its close. Avoid losing that vote and the recorded loss is cleared; lose it too and your term ends.
  • If you do not answer, the board's offer counts as accepted in the first weeks of the new season.
  • Two lost ordinary assemblies in a row end your term as chairman.

Extraordinary assembly

If fan approval is below 30 and board trust below 40 as the last two weeks begin, an extraordinary assembly meets at the season close. It never happens in your first season, or in two seasons in a row.

Losing an extraordinary assembly never ends your term: the new season's targets become demanding instead, and the ordinary calendar stays as it is.

How to prepare

  • Fan approval carries the most weight. Wins and derby results move it most; cup runs, investment in training or youth and a new stand help too, while aggressive ticket prices and closed outlets cost points.
  • Keep an eye on the season objectives, since they make up board trust.
  • Look after city hall and the business community, not only the terraces. See Stakeholders.
