Short answers about your role, saves, ads, purchases and languages.

Data and purchases

There is no Büyük Başkan account; saves and achievements are stored on the device. If you sign in with Google Play Games you can also save your career to the cloud (Cloud save on the save screen; Automatic cloud backup is off by default) and collect your achievements in your Play Games profile. The Android build may optionally use Google Play Billing and AdMob.

The Android build uses the AdMob and Google Play Billing SDKs; the privacy policy, terms and Data Safety declaration cover them.

  • Saves and achievements can be lost if device app data is removed; to keep a copy, use the Google Play Games cloud save.
  • There are no interstitial ads, so there is no ad-free version to buy.
  • The store Super Stadium is built for the current club at once; it is not carried to new careers.
  • You can change your ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy options (shown when Google requires the consent form).

Your role

Do I pick the team and the tactics? No. You are the chairman, not the coach. The manager runs the matches and the training. You appoint and sack managers, answer their requests, and run the money, transfers, stadium and facilities.

Can I be sacked? Yes, in two ways: lose two ordinary General Assembly votes in a row, or go bankrupt a second time (the first bankruptcy brings a one-off trustee rescue, see Finance). You can also resign from the bottom section of Settings, which ends the career.

Can good relationships buy results? Never. No relationship ever changes a match result, a referee decision, a fine or a fine reduction, and no assembly option buys a vote: only what you did counts. See Stakeholders.

Saves

Where are my saves kept? On your device. Settings > Save files shows your save points and makes new ones; Load game opens an older one. There is no developer server. One save per season is enough, and it keeps you eligible for the Highscores; save more than once in a season and the save icon greys out.

Can I edit my save file? No. The game checks that the career on your device matches what it last saved. If a career was changed outside the game, or is damaged, it will not open. You can carry on from a save point that checks out, start a new career, or bring back a cloud save from the load screen.

How does the cloud save work? Each career has its own cloud save, and the cloud holds up to five careers. Saving a career again updates its cloud save; Load from cloud brings it back to the device.

Does starting a new career delete my saves? No. A new career replaces the one that is open, and progress since your last save is lost, but your saved slots are kept.

Ads and purchases

Are there ads? Only rewarded videos, up to 10 a season, and only when you start one.

What do the purchases do? Backing packs add money to the current career, and the fans and the manager trust you more. If a paid purchase did not arrive, use Restore purchases in Settings.

Offline play and languages

Does the game work offline? Yes. Rewarded videos and online features such as cloud save need a connection.

Which languages are there? Turkish, English, German, Spanish, Portuguese and French. You can change the language in Settings.
