Data and purchases

There is no Büyük Başkan account in the current build; saves and achievements are stored locally on the device only. The Android build may optionally use Google Play Billing and AdMob.

The Android build uses the AdMob and Google Play Billing SDKs; the privacy policy, terms and Data Safety declaration cover them.

  • Saves and achievements can be lost if device app data is removed; regular device backup is recommended.
  • There are no interstitial ads, so there is no ad-free version to buy.
  • The store Super Stadium is built for the current club at once; it is not carried to new careers.
  • You can change your ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy options (shown when Google requires the consent form).
