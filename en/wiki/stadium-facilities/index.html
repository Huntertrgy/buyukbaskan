Stadium

Seats cap your ticket income, and the Stadium screen's Stands, Income and Investments tabs are where you grow them.

Back to Wiki

Stands

Your ground has four side stands (north, east, south, west), the corners and the pitch. Pick one on the Stands tab to see its capacity and the next step's cost, time and speed. Expand opens the stand's sheet, where you choose a target capacity.

  • The stand stays closed while it is built; the new capacity opens when the work is finished.
  • You can pay extra to build faster. One build runs on a stand at a time.
  • Once a stand is big enough you can rename it.
  • Your relationship with city hall moves stand construction cost by up to 5%.
  • Pick the pitch and Improve to relay it straight away.

The expansion ladder

Side stands grow in steps: 100 seats at a time under 200, 200 at a time up to 1,000, 500 at a time up to 5,000, then 1,000 at a time up to a full size of 25,000.

The corners have two steps, 4,000 and 8,000 seats. Every side stand must hold at least 6,000 before the corners can reach 4,000, and at least 10,000 before they can reach 8,000. A finished stand also lifts the base crowd: by 3% above 9,000 seats, by 6% above 15,000.

The stadium picture grows with your stands: a partial roof from 3,000 seats, a full roof from 8,000, floodlights from 5,000. There is no separate roof or floodlight purchase. The seat pattern and roof colour are for looks only.

Income outlets

On the Income tab you open and close units of food and drink, the club shop, parking, the VIP lounge and the museum. The medical centre opens from the rehabilitation building at the training ground.

  • Outlets need a minimum capacity; the VIP lounge also needs premium stands (VIP boxes, or a stand of 10,000 or more).
  • Parking raises matchday attendance. The VIP lounge and museum raise new sponsor offers.
  • Closing a unit is immediate, gives no refund and costs fan approval.

Stadium modules and naming deals

The Investments tab holds three stadium modules: advertising boards (board income per home match), pitch structure (less post-match wear) and scoreboard and sound. You pay now; a module opens when construction ends, and its upkeep starts then. Your league tier caps the level; promotion unlocks more.

In the professional leagues, 2 stands (4 in the Süper Lig) can carry a naming sponsor. Offers come in week 94; accepting costs fan approval at first. See Finance.

The Super Stadium

Normal expansion stops short of 120,000 seats; only the Super Stadium reaches it. It rebuilds the ground as four 28,000-seat side stands plus 8,000 in the corners, 120,000 seats in all, with a pitch of 99.

Buy it with game money on the Investments tab, or through Google Play (built for your current club only).

  • It is ready at once and cheaper to run.
  • It brings the stadium modules to level 2 as far as your league tier allows, with no upkeep on those modules.
  • Builds in progress end at once at Super Stadium size, without a refund. Food and shop outlets stay as they are.
