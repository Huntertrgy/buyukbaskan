Stadium and Facilities

Stadium and facility investments affect income, club value, and long-term growth.

Back to Wiki

Investment choice

Stand, VIP, pitch, and commercial upgrades cost cash now; timed well, they strengthen club value later.

The Super Stadium bought in the store is built for the current club at once; it is not carried to new careers.

  • Do not make major upgrades with unsafe cash.
  • Tie high-income upgrades to the season plan.
  • The store Super Stadium is a consumable: it builds the current club's stadium once.
