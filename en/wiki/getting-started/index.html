Getting Started

Your first job as chairman is to found the club, set what the board will ask of you and get through your first weeks.

Back to Wiki

Founding your club

Tap New game on the opening screen and work through the steps with the button at the bottom. Stuck on any screen? Tap the question mark at the top left to learn what it does.

  • Club: give your club a name (3-14 characters), its stadium a name (3-25 characters) and enter its home city. The city places the club on the map and decides the league group you play in.
  • Kit: choose your kit colours, pattern and crest. Leave them and a design based on the club name is used.
  • Chairman: enter your first and last name, then pick a portrait or add your own photo (PNG, JPG or WebP under 5 MB). Your name appears in the news and in your career history.
  • Rivals: choose up to 5 rivals. Clubs marked "Same group" give you a derby this season. Skip the step and the game picks nearby clubs for you.
  • Charter: the foundation charter shows your club, stadium, city, you and your rivals together. Sign and seal starts the career.

Chairman style

The charter has a fold called "Style and demands". It sets what the board will ask of you, and you can change both later on the Chairman Career screen (changes apply from the next season).

Chairman style sets the weights on your season report, not the game rules.

  • Balanced: league 40%, cups 20%, cash 20%, fans 20%.
  • Ambitious Builder: league 50%, cups 25%, cash 10%, fans 15%.
  • Prudent Steward: league 30%, cups 10%, cash 40%, fans 20%.
  • The People's Chairman: league 30%, cups 20%, cash 10%, fans 40%.

Board demands

Board demands set how tight the targets are.

  • Relaxed (recommended): room on the league target, fans at 40%, cash above the credit limit.
  • Standard: the place the fans expect, fans at 50%, spend at most 40% of your cash.
  • Demanding: above expectation, fans at 60%, cash must not shrink.

Where you start

A new club starts in the 1. Amatör (First Amateur), the lowest league in the pyramid, in either the West or the East group. The Seasons, Promotion and Cups page shows the climb ahead.

Your first weeks

  • A short tour shows the news, the home screen and the facility notes. You can replay it later.
  • The first-season guide gives you five goals, each worth prestige: appoint a permanent manager, set the ticket price, sign at least one player, bring training up to squad level, and build a bond with the fans.
  • The dugout needs a manager first. Candidates are rated on matches, coaching and transfers. Appoint Recommended hires the best candidate who costs no compensation; picking one of the first two on the list costs 52 weeks of his wage.
  • The ticket price decision arrives as a news card in week 1.
  • Once a manager is in place he may suggest a first signing for the squad's weakest position. The decision is yours.
  • In season one, an unanswered friendly, bonus, sponsor or ticket card takes the recommended option.
