First setup

A new career defines the club, chairman name, stadium name, language, and presentation preferences. These choices become the base of the career save.

Checking squad, budget, and target information in the first week makes later decisions safer.

  • Review club and rivals.
  • Check squad age, wage load, and quality spread.
