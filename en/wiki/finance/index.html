Cash balance

Wages, transfer spend, and stadium investment directly affect the club's cash position.

If rewarded-ad support is added later, small cash help should be framed in the game world; the current build does not actively use that SDK.

  • Check wage budget before transfers.
  • Balance large facility work against match income.
  • Use recurring income growth to escape negative cash.

Get Investment

Finance > Get Investment offers rewarded videos (up to 10 a season, the reward grows with the division), three investor cash packs and the Super Stadium through Google Play Billing.

Rewarded videos and purchases work in the Android app only. A paid purchase that was not added yet is finished from Finance or Settings > Restore purchases.

  • Investor packs: a fixed amount for the bank, and the fans and the manager trust the chairman more.
  • Super Stadium: built at once for the current club; it can also be bought with in-game money.
  • Rewarded video: a reward set by the club's division, at most 10 a season.
  • In-game copy can frame offers as municipal support, government aid, supporter donations, or sponsor investment.
