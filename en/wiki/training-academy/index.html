Training Ground and Youth Academy

Your club has two facility sites, the training ground and the youth academy, and both work the same way on the Facilities screen.

Back to Wiki

Why they matter

  • Training ground: if the training level is below a player's rating he loses rating and you lose approval; above it, players under 32 improve. Training at squad level keeps players improving, and the screen tells you how many squad players are above the current ceiling.
  • Youth academy: the academy brings new young players into the squad, and youngsters grow towards its rating. When the manager rates a youngster as ready for the first team, he can offer him a professional deal. The yearly intake joins in week 102. Open the youth squad to see your prospects.

Building

Select a building on the map or in the list to see its next level, points, cost and duration.

Training ground buildings: main pitch, training pitches, main building, analysis tower, performance block, rehabilitation and campus. Youth academy buildings: academy pitches, academy building, scouting and development centre, performance block, school building, campus and showcase pitch.

  • You pay now; the facility rating rises when the building opens.
  • Each site has one construction slot, so one build at a time per site.
  • Choose Normal or Fast speed; Fast costs 1.4 times as much and takes about half the time.
  • Some levels need a minimum facility rating before you can start them.
  • Sites wear over time. Maintenance removes the wear and restores the points.
  • Closing a level gives no refund, and specialist effects that depend on it pause until the building reopens.

Rating and tiers

Each site has a facility rating out of 99 and a named tier. The first time a site reaches a new tier, the news celebrates it and fan approval rises by 2. Wear and repair never repeat the bonus.

  • Local: from 10.
  • Regional: from 50.
  • National: from 70.
  • Elite: from 85.
  • World: from 97.

Specialist effects

Some levels also carry a specialist effect on top of the rating. Your league tier holds some effect steps back. Build them anyway and promotion turns them on.

  • Training: fitness (the weekly fitness gain reaches players a little above the training level), analysis (a monthly chance of an extra session for a player below the level, never lifting him above it) and rehabilitation (random injuries are shorter).
  • Academy: dormitory (a bigger youth squad at the week-102 intake), education (a better monthly chance for youths below the cap to improve) and youth pitch (shorter injuries for players aged 21 or under).

Medical centre

The medical centre opens from the rehabilitation building's sheet. It needs a minimum stadium capacity or training ground rating, and it shortens injury lay-offs.

Backroom staff

From the Managers screen you can hire and fire backroom staff: assistant, physiotherapist, coach and scout. Assistants and physios raise team chemistry; a coach makes your own players' potential read sharper; a scout does the same for other clubs' players.

Each hire adds to one of the manager's ratings. Lower divisions have no backroom staff, and more slots open as you climb.
