Transfers and Contracts

You negotiate every transfer yourself, and every signing comes out of the club's account.

Back to Wiki

Transfer windows

Deals can only be done while a window is open. The summer window, from the end of the season to week 10, counts as one window; the January window ends in week 54.

The last week of a window is deadline day: when it ends, unfinished deals and unanswered bids fall through.

Signing a player

A signing has two rounds of talks:

  • Fee talks with the selling club: you make an opening offer and they accept, counter, or walk out if pushed too far. Watch the room mood and the rounds left. You can offer a sell-on clause (a cut of a future sale) so they accept less cash today.
  • Personal terms with the player and his agent: weekly wage, contract length, signing bonus and, if you like, a release clause. A club that pays the release clause takes the player without your consent. Some players insist on a clause, and demanding players won't discuss a low wage. Agent commission is part of the cost.
  • Free agents skip the fee and go straight to personal terms. A loan signing runs to the end of the season, and you pay the wages.
  • The Fit indicator reads squad need, playing style, profile, character and abilities together, with the manager's comment.

How much you can spend

A bid can never be more than the lowest of three numbers: your transfer budget, your cash plus overdraft, and what is left of this window's spending limit.

  • The spending limit is a licensing rule: half of the latest season's income, or a floor when that is lower.
  • Every sale raises it. Agent commission and signing bonuses count against it; loans and free transfers do not.
  • The counter starts again with each window.

Foreign players

The Süper Lig allows up to 14 foreigners, of whom at most 10 may be older than 22. The 1. Lig allows up to 8, at most 6 of them older than 22. Below that, foreign players cannot be fielded.

If relegation leaves you over the quota at the end of the season, the excess is sold abroad.

Selling and loaning out

Answer bids in the Transfer Hub under Sales: accept, or open talks and ask for more. The buyer may raise the bid, make a final offer or leave. Loan offers are not negotiated: accept or reject.

A loaned player stays registered to you and comes back when the loan ends, and a player under 22 can improve while away. Accepting every bid wears the squad down, and the game warns you before a sale takes the squad below 18 players.

Contracts

  • A player whose contract runs out leaves for free.
  • Players ask for new deals and the manager recommends renewals. Refusing upsets the player, and the manager too if he asked.
  • The contract desk collects each season's expiring contracts in one card.
  • From a player's profile you can discuss a new contract, put him on the transfer list, make him available for loan, or release him. Releasing a player means paying a settlement.
  • If the club is relegated, the relegation clause in the contracts cuts first-team wages by 20%.
