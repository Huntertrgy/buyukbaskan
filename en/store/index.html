Full description

Being a chairman in Türkiye

Büyük Başkan is a management game about being the chairman of a football club in Türkiye. Your place is not on the touchline but at the club's decision desk.

Found your club: pick its name, colours, kit, crest and stadium, and sign the foundation file. Start in the amateur leagues and climb Türkiye's football pyramid step by step, from the Bölgesel Amatör to the 3. Lig and from the 1. Lig to the Süper Lig. Draw the big clubs in the Turkish Cup and earn a European ticket.

Every week new files land on your desk: the manager's squad requests, reporters' questions, the stands' mood, the bank's warning. You make the call, and you see what it does in the news, in the stands and in the bank.

The club is not yours alone. At the genel kurul (general assembly) you face the club's members, who vote on your annual report and your budget; win their approval and your mandate is renewed, lose it and your chair is at risk. You manage your ties with the terraces, the press, the pundits, the city hall, the business world and the federation. Good relations open doors, but no relationship ever buys a referee decision, a match result or a vote.

Real clubs appear in the game by name only; no crests, logos or sponsor names are used. Büyük Başkan is not affiliated with these clubs, the leagues or the federation.

  • A weekly decision loop: news, meetings, crises
  • The general assembly: the vote on your report and budget, extraordinary assemblies
  • Six stakeholders: terraces, press, pundits, city hall, business, federation
  • Transfer negotiations, contracts and the spending limit
  • Stadium, facility and youth investments
  • Matchday: live match flow, crowd and income
  • The Turkish Cup, the Super Cup and the European cups
  • Saves stay on your device; no account needed
Data Safety

Current build declaration

No personal data is collected directly by the developer; career data stays on the device by default.

The Android build includes active Google AdMob ads and optional Google Play Billing. AdMob may process advertising ID and device identifiers on Google's side for ad delivery. Career saves and achievements remain on the device only.

  • No Büyük Başkan account.
  • No automatic personal-data transfer to the developer.
  • AdMob: advertising ID / device identifiers (processed by Google).
  • Rewarded videos only, no interstitial ads; Billing opens once the Play Console products are live.
  • Career saves and achievements stored locally on the device only.
  • Optional Google Play Billing and AdMob on Android.
  • Android manifest permission surface: INTERNET, AD_ID.
  • Ad consent: Google UMP consent form where Google requires it; changed in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Sentry crash reports: optional, off by default, no personal data.
Monetization

Finance > Get Investment

The Finance > Get Investment section includes rewarded videos on Android. Google Play Billing products (`cash_pack_local_investor`, `cash_pack_tycoon`, `cash_pack_billionaire`, `super_stadium_build`, all consumable) are defined in code; purchases open once the products are active in Play Console.

  • Rewarded videos: at most 10 a season, the reward set by the division.
  • No interstitial ads.
  • Billing: restore, refund/revocation, and duplicate-grant tests required.
  • Declare third-party ad data (Google AdMob) in the Data Safety form.
