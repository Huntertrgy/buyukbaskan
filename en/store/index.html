Full description

Being a football club chairman in Türkiye

Büyük Başkan is a chairman game set in Turkish football: you run a football club from the boardroom. It differs from a football manager game in where you sit: at the club chairman's desk. You leave the pitch to the manager and discuss the formation with them; as football club chairman you run the money, the transfers and the stadium.

Your first job as football club chairman is to found the club: pick its name, colours, kit, crest and stadium, and sign the foundation file. You start a small football club in the amateur leagues of Turkish football and climb an eight-league pyramid step by step, from the Bölgesel Amatör to the 3. Lig and from the 1. Lig to the Süper Lig. You draw the big clubs in the Turkish Cup and play for the Süper Lig title and the European cups.

Every week the news of Turkish football lands on your desk: the manager's squad requests, transfer offers, reporters' questions, the stands' mood, the bank's warning. In this chairman game you negotiate every transfer yourself; as in any football manager game, each signing has a price, and it comes out of the club's account. You make the call as club chairman, and you see what it does in the news, in the stands and in the bank.

Being a football club chairman does not make the club yours alone. At the genel kurul (general assembly) you face the club's members, who vote on your annual report and your budget; win their approval and your mandate is renewed, lose it and your chair is at risk. You manage your ties with the terraces, the press, the pundits, the city hall, the business world and the federation. Good relations open doors, but no relationship ever buys a referee decision, a match result or a vote.

Real clubs appear in the game by name only; no crests, logos or sponsor names are used. Büyük Başkan is not affiliated with these clubs, the Süper Lig or the other leagues, or the federation.

  • A weekly decision loop: news, meetings, crises
  • The general assembly: the vote on your report and budget, extraordinary assemblies
  • Six stakeholders: terraces, press, pundits, city hall, business, federation
  • Transfer negotiations, contracts and the spending limit
  • Stadium, facility and youth investments
  • Matchday: live match flow, crowd and income
  • The Turkish Cup, the Super Cup and the European cups
  • Career save on your device, with optional Google Play Games cloud backup
  • Google Play Games achievements
  • Light and dark theme
  • Six languages: Turkish, English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French
  • Rewarded videos only when you choose; no interstitial ads
  • Plays offline
Data Safety

Current build declaration

No personal data is collected directly by the developer; career data stays on the device by default.

The Android build includes active Google AdMob ads and optional Google Play Billing. AdMob may process advertising ID and device identifiers on Google's side for ad delivery. Career saves and achievements stay on the device unless the player uses Google Play Games: cloud save (optional, off by default) stores the career save in the player's Google account, and achievements are unlocked in the player's Play Games profile. Nothing is sent to a developer server.

  • No Büyük Başkan account.
  • No automatic personal-data transfer to the developer.
  • AdMob: advertising ID / device identifiers (processed by Google).
  • Rewarded videos only, no interstitial ads; purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Career saves and achievements stored on the device; optional Google Play Games cloud save (off by default) and achievements are held by Google.
  • Optional Google Play Billing and AdMob on Android.
  • Android permissions (release build, including those the libraries add): INTERNET, AD_ID, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID / ATTRIBUTION / TOPICS, com.android.vending.BILLING, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, VIBRATE.
  • Ad consent: Google UMP consent form where Google requires it; changed in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Firebase Crashlytics crash reports: optional, off by default; crash log, device model and an installation id.
  • Before each release, re-check the Data Safety form against Google Play Games Services (Saved Games, achievements).
Monetization

Raise Funds screen

The Raise Funds screen (opened from the Funds tile on the home screen or from Finances) includes rewarded videos on Android. The products (`cash_pack_local_investor`, `cash_pack_tycoon`, `cash_pack_billionaire`, `super_stadium_build`, all consumable) are sold through Google Play Billing.

  • Rewarded videos: at most 10 a season, the reward set by the division.
  • No interstitial ads.
  • Billing: restore, refund/revocation, and duplicate-grant tests required.
  • Declare third-party ad data (Google AdMob) in the Data Safety form.
