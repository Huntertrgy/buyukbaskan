The user agrees to use the app according to applicable store rules and local law.

Purchases and ads

The Android build includes AdMob rewarded videos and no interstitial ads. Watching is optional; rewarded videos launch only from Finance > Get Investment.

In-app purchases are consumable and run through Google Play Billing: each one is added to the current career once. Purchase and refund flows follow Google Play rules.

  • Google Play purchase and refund flows run through Google's payment system.
  • Rewarded ad viewing is optional.
  • There are no interstitial ads.
  • In-game cash packs only affect the simulation balance; they do not represent real money or real financial value.
  • There are no social follow or share rewards.
